Whether you’ve led an institutional initiative, uncovered new research, developed an approach worth sharing, or learned valuable lessons along the way, we’d love to hear from you.

Share your expertise with peers working to help institutions prepare students for the workplace—and the hiring process that gets them there.

Why it matters

Your Experience Could Help Your Peers

Across higher education, teams are working to connect academic learning with career opportunities, engage students in career preparation, and understand what leads to stronger outcomes.

The WriteWay Webinar Series gives those teams a place to learn from one another.

We welcome thoughtful perspectives, practical examples, and honest conversations about what worked, what didn’t, and what others could learn from the experience.

Session ideas

What Could You Bring to the Conversation?

We’re particularly interested in sessions that offer actionable insights, real-world examples, and fresh perspectives on helping institutions prepare students for success beyond the classroom.

Topics could include:

  1. Embedding career readiness across campus

    Connecting coursework, career coaching, experiential learning, and student support.

  2. Engaging students in career preparation

    Reaching students earlier and helping them make meaningful use of available resources.

  3. Strengthening employer and alumni partnerships

    Creating connections that support learning, professional development, and employment opportunities.

  4. Using AI and technology effectively

    Sharing practical applications, implementation lessons, and approaches to campus collaboration.

  5. Measuring readiness and career outcomes

    Using research, assessment, and reporting to understand progress and guide decisions.

  6. Leading institutional change

    Building support, coordinating departments, and putting career readiness strategies into practice.

Have a relevant topic beyond these areas? We welcome proposals that bring a clear perspective and useful takeaways for higher education professionals.
